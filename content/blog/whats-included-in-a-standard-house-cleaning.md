Booking a cleaning is easier when you know exactly what you are getting. A standard house cleaning follows a consistent, room-by-room routine focused on everyday upkeep. Here is what that looks like, room by room, so there are no surprises on the day.

## The kitchen

The kitchen sees the most daily use, so it gets careful attention in every standard clean.

- Wiping down countertops and backsplashes
- Cleaning the stovetop exterior and the outside of appliances
- Scrubbing and sanitizing the sink
- Wiping cabinet fronts and reachable surfaces
- Emptying the trash and sweeping or mopping the floor

Interior appliance cleaning, like inside the oven or fridge, is detailed work that typically falls outside a standard visit. Just ask if you want it added.

## Bathrooms

Bathrooms are where sanitizing matters most, and a standard clean covers them thoroughly.

- Cleaning and disinfecting the toilet inside and out
- Scrubbing the tub, shower, and sink
- Wiping mirrors and reachable surfaces
- Polishing fixtures and faucets
- Emptying trash and mopping the floor

## Bedrooms and living areas

These rooms get the upkeep that keeps your home feeling fresh and dust-free.

- Dusting surfaces, shelves, and furniture
- Tidying and straightening reachable areas
- Vacuuming carpets and rugs
- Sweeping and mopping hard floors
- Emptying wastebaskets

Making beds and changing linens can usually be included on request, just let the crew know your preference.

## Throughout the home

Some tasks apply everywhere, and a standard clean handles them across the whole space.

- Dusting reachable surfaces and ledges
- Wiping light switches and door handles
- Spot-cleaning visible marks on surfaces
- Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout

## What's not in a standard clean

Knowing what is excluded is just as useful. The following are detailed tasks that typically belong to a deep clean or come as add-ons:

- Baseboards, trim, and detailed grout work
- Interior windows and inside appliances
- Behind and under heavy furniture and appliances
- Vents, ceiling fans, and high light fixtures

If your home needs that level of detail, a deep clean is the better starting point. Our guide on [standard clean vs. deep clean](/blog/standard-clean-vs-deep-clean) explains the difference and helps you pick the right one.
