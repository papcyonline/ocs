There is nothing quite like the first night in a new home, and you deserve to spend it somewhere genuinely clean. A move-in cleaning, done while the place is still empty, is the single best way to start fresh. Before the boxes arrive, every surface is reachable and nothing is in your way.

## Why timing the clean to an empty home matters

Once your furniture and boxes are in place, the hardest-to-reach spots become nearly impossible to clean well. An empty home is a rare opportunity. You can wipe the backs of closets, reach into every cabinet, and clean the floors wall to wall without moving a thing.

Even a home that looks spotless can hide the previous occupant's dust, residue, and grime in places you would never check during a walkthrough. Starting with a thorough clean means the home is truly yours from day one.

## What to cover before you unpack

Work through an empty home methodically, top to bottom. Here is what deserves attention:

### Kitchen
- Inside and outside of every cabinet and drawer
- Inside the oven, refrigerator, and microwave
- Behind and under where appliances will sit
- Sink, faucet, and countertops
- Floors, including corners and edges

### Bathrooms
- Toilet, tub, shower, and tile inside and out
- Inside vanities, cabinets, and medicine cabinets
- Mirrors, fixtures, and exhaust fans
- Floors and grout

### Bedrooms, living areas, and throughout
- Closet shelves and rods
- Baseboards, door frames, and switch plates
- Light fixtures and ceiling fans
- Windows, sills, and tracks
- All floors, vacuumed and mopped

## Do not forget the high-touch surfaces

In a home that has changed hands, the things people touch most are worth special care. Wipe down and disinfect:

1. Doorknobs and handles
2. Light switches
3. Cabinet and drawer pulls
4. Railings and bannisters
5. Thermostats and remotes

These small surfaces carry the most residue from previous occupants, and they take only minutes to refresh once the home is empty.
