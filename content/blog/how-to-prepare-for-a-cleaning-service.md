A little preparation goes a long way. When your cleaning crew can get straight to work, they spend their time on what actually matters, the cleaning itself, and you get a more thorough result. None of this takes long, and none of it involves cleaning ahead of time.

## Clear the clutter first

Cleaners clean. They are not there to sort your mail or organize the kids' toys. The single most helpful thing you can do is pick up loose items so surfaces are clear and reachable.

- Put away clothes, toys, and stray belongings
- Clear counters, tables, and floors of clutter
- Stack and remove dishes from sinks if you can
- Tidy paperwork and small items off surfaces

Every minute the crew is not spent moving your things is a minute spent making your home cleaner.

## Make spaces accessible

The crew can only clean what they can reach. A quick walk through your home with access in mind makes a real difference.

- Unlock gates, doors, or rooms you want cleaned
- Move light obstacles away from baseboards and corners
- Clear a path to areas like laundry rooms or back bathrooms
- Note anything fragile or off-limits so it can be avoided

## Handle pets and people

A calm, accessible home helps everyone. Decide in advance how you want to manage pets and household members during the visit.

- Secure pets in a comfortable, separate space
- Let the crew know about any animals before they arrive
- If kids are home, keep play areas clear of the rooms being cleaned

Our supplies are eco-friendly and pet- and kid-safe, so you do not need to worry about what is being used around your family. Securing pets is simply about keeping them comfortable and letting the team move efficiently.

## Point out priorities and problem spots

You know your home better than anyone. A short note or quick conversation about your priorities helps the crew focus where it counts.

- Flag high-traffic areas or rooms that need extra attention
- Mention any stains, buildup, or trouble spots
- Share preferences, like a product to avoid on a certain surface
- Tell them what success looks like for you that day

If you are not satisfied with any area, our 24-hour re-clean guarantee means we return free within 24 hours to make it right, so do not hesitate to be specific about what matters most.

## Sort out access and logistics

If you will not be home, settle the practical details ahead of time so there are no surprises on the day.

- Arrange a key, code, or entry instructions
- Confirm where to park and how to enter
- Leave a contact number in case questions come up
- Note your hours, our crews work Monday through Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM

## A quick pre-clean checklist

Run through this before the crew arrives:

1. Clutter cleared from surfaces and floors
2. Rooms and entry points accessible
3. Pets secured and noted
4. Priorities and problem spots flagged
5. Access, parking, and contact details confirmed
