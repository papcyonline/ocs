Professional cleaners don't scrub harder than you, they just work smarter: the right order, the right dwell time, and a habit of letting products do the work. Here's how to clean a bathroom the way a pro does, top to bottom, with nothing missed.

## Start the soak, then walk away

The first move is to apply your cleaners and let them sit while you do everything else. This is the single biggest difference between an okay clean and a pro one.

- Spray or apply cleaner inside the toilet bowl
- Coat the tub, shower walls, and glass with a soap-scum or descaling cleaner
- Hit any visible buildup on fixtures

Give these several minutes. While they work, you'll clean the rest of the room and come back to surfaces that wipe clean instead of fighting you.

## Work from the top down

Dust and spray drift downward, so always clean high before low:

1. **Vents, light fixtures, and the exhaust fan cover** — dust collects here and recirculates
2. **Mirror and any glass** — streak-free while the room is still dry
3. **Cabinets, counters, and the vanity** — clear them and wipe fully
4. **Sink and faucet** — scrub the basin and descale the fixtures

By the time you reach the soaking surfaces, the cleaners have done the hard part.

## The fixtures: toilet, tub, and shower

Now circle back to what's been soaking:

- **Toilet** — scrub the bowl with the brush, then wipe the tank, lid, seat, base, and behind it, where odors hide
- **Tub and shower** — scrub walls and floor, then focus on grout lines and corners
- **Glass and doors** — squeegee or wipe for a clear, spot-free finish
- **Showerhead** — descale to restore water flow and shine

### Grout and soap scum

These two are where bathrooms look tired. For grout, apply a cleaner, let it dwell, then work the lines with a stiff brush. For soap scum and hard water film, the secret is patience: let an acidic or descaling cleaner sit and break the buildup down before you scrub. Mineral deposits on glass and chrome respond far better to dwell time than elbow grease.

## Finish with floors and final details

End at floor level so nothing lands on a surface you've already done. Sweep, then mop, getting into corners and behind the toilet. Then the finishing touches that signal a true pro clean:

- Wipe baseboards and the base of the toilet
- Disinfect high-touch spots: light switch, door handle, faucet handles, flush lever
- Replace towels and restock toilet paper and soap
- Empty and wipe the trash can

A bathroom cleaned in this order feels genuinely fresh, not just sprayed over.
