There is a reason the phrase "spring cleaning" stuck around. Timing a deep clean to the right moment means your effort lasts longer and your home feels its best when it matters most. So when is the best time to deep clean your home? The answer depends on how the seasons treat your house here in greater Louisville.

## Spring: clear out the winter

After months of closed windows and running heat, indoor air goes stale and a fine layer of dust settles on nearly everything. Spring is the classic deep-clean window for good reason. As the weather warms, you can open the house up, air it out, and tackle the buildup that winter left behind.

Spring is ideal for:

- Washing windows and letting natural light back in
- Clearing dust from vents, fans, and baseboards
- Refreshing carpets and upholstery that trapped winter grime
- Wiping down walls and high-touch surfaces

A spring deep clean sets a clean baseline right as you start spending more time outdoors and entertaining guests.

## Fall: prepare to seal up

The other strong contender is autumn. Before you close the windows and run the heat again, a fall deep clean gets your home ready to be sealed up for months. Clean vents and ducts mean your heating system pushes cleaner air. A scrubbed kitchen is ready for holiday cooking, and fresh bedding and upholstery make the cozy months genuinely cozy.

Fall is also the season many households host gatherings, so a deep clean ahead of the holidays takes pressure off later.

## Summer and winter have their place too

You do not have to wait for spring or fall. Each season offers its own reason:

| Season | Why it works |
| --- | --- |
| Spring | Clears winter dust and stale air |
| Summer | Easy ventilation; great for tackling one big project at a time |
| Fall | Resets the home before the windows close |
| Winter | Quieter schedules and a fresh start before the new year |

Summer is excellent for slow, focused projects since you can open up the house and work room by room. Winter, with its quieter calendars, is a fine time to reset before a new year.

## How to know your home is due

Seasons aside, your house gives you signals. Consider a deep clean when you notice:

1. Dust returning faster than usual after a regular clean
2. Grime building up in grout, tracks, and corners
3. Lingering odors that a surface wipe does not fix
4. Appliances and vents looking dull or coated
