If you have ever booked a cleaning and wondered why one option costs more or takes longer than another, the answer usually comes down to two words: standard and deep. Knowing the difference helps you book the right service the first time and avoid paying for more, or less, than your home needs.

## What a standard clean covers

A standard clean is your regular upkeep. It keeps a home that is already in reasonable shape looking and feeling fresh. Think of it as the maintenance layer you repeat on a schedule.

A typical standard clean includes:

- Dusting reachable surfaces, shelves, and furniture
- Vacuuming and mopping floors throughout
- Wiping kitchen counters, the stovetop exterior, and the sink
- Cleaning and sanitizing bathroom sinks, toilets, tubs, and mirrors
- Emptying trash and general tidying

It is efficient by design. Because it focuses on the surfaces you use every day, a standard clean is the natural fit for recurring service.

## What a deep clean adds

A deep clean does everything a standard clean does, then goes further into the spots that build up slowly and rarely get attention. It is more thorough and more time-intensive.

A deep clean typically reaches:

- Baseboards, door frames, and trim
- Tile grout and tough soap scum
- Behind and around appliances where dust collects
- Vents, light fixtures, and ceiling fans
- Interior windowsills and detailed corners

The goal is to reset a home, clearing accumulated grime so that ongoing standard cleans can keep it that way.

## Standard vs. deep at a glance

| Feature | Standard clean | Deep clean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Ongoing upkeep | Resetting buildup |
| Frequency | Recurring | Occasional |
| Detail level | Everyday surfaces | Hidden and overlooked areas |
| Time required | Shorter | Longer |

## When to choose which

Reach for a **deep clean** when:

- It has been a while since the last thorough cleaning
- You are starting a new recurring plan and want a fresh baseline
- You are moving in or out, or recovering from a busy season
- Allergies, pets, or dust have you noticing buildup

Stick with a **standard clean** when your home is already maintained and you simply want to keep it that way. ## How pricing differs

A few factors drive the cost difference between the two. Square footage, the number of bathrooms, the current condition of the home, and how much detailed work is involved all play a role. A deep clean covers more ground in finer detail, so it naturally runs higher than a standard visit. The good news: once you have had that initial reset, booking recurring standard cleans on a weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly schedule saves 20 percent compared with one-time rates, and it keeps deep-clean buildup from ever returning.
