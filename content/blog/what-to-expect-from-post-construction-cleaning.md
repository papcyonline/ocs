A finished build looks done — until the light hits it. Construction leaves behind a layer of ultra-fine drywall dust that settles into every surface, vent, and crevice, plus debris, adhesive residue, stickers, and paint flecks that ordinary cleaning isn't built to handle. **Post-construction cleaning** is the specialized step that turns a completed site into a space that's actually ready to use.

Here's what the process involves and how to plan for it.

## Why construction dust is different

Drywall and sanding dust is far finer than household dust. It travels through the air, resettles after you wipe it, and clogs HVAC filters fast. Removing it properly takes the right sequence, the right tools (HEPA vacuums, microfiber, not a shop broom), and more than one pass. Skipping it means tenants and homeowners are still finding grit weeks after move-in.

## The three phases

Professional post-construction cleaning is usually staged to match the build timeline:

### 1. Rough clean

Done while work is still wrapping up. The crew clears large debris, removes trash and leftover materials, and does an initial sweep so the space is workable for final trades. This keeps the site safe and on schedule.

### 2. Final clean

The main event, once construction is complete. This is the detailed pass:

- **Fine dust removal** from every surface — walls, ceilings, ledges, fixtures
- **Window, frame, and glass** detailing, including stickers and adhesive
- **Cabinet, drawer, and closet** interiors
- **Fixtures, outlets, and switch plates** wiped down
- **Floors** prepped — vacuumed, mopped, or buffed depending on material

### 3. Touch-up / terminal clean

A final pass just before handover or move-in, catching any dust that resettled and any marks left by final walk-throughs. This is what makes the space genuinely **punch-list ready**.

## What's included in a professional job

A complete [post-construction clean](/services/post-construction-cleaning) covers:

- Debris removal and haul-off
- Fine construction-dust removal from all surfaces
- Window and glass detailing
- Fixture, trim, and cabinet wipe-down

## How to plan it

- **Book early.** Schedule the clean around your handover date so the crew can stage the phases.
- **Request a walkthrough.** For larger sites, an on-site walkthrough gives you an accurate scope and timeline.
- **Confirm insurance.** Job-site work should be done by an insured, bonded crew — ask for proof.
