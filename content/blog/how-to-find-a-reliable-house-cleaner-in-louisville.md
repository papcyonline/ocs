Letting someone into your home takes trust, so finding a house cleaner in Louisville is about much more than picking the first name that pops up. A little vetting up front saves you from headaches later and helps you find a service you can rely on for years.

## Start with credentials, not just price

The most important questions have nothing to do with the rate. Before anything else, confirm the basics that protect you and your home:

- **Insured and bonded.** Insurance covers accidental damage or injury during a visit. Bonding protects you against theft. A serious company carries both, and Ottri Cleaning Services is insured, bonded, and DBE certified.
- **Background-checked crews.** You want to know who is walking through your door. Ask directly whether cleaners are screened. Ours are background-checked.
- **Clear scope of work.** A professional can tell you exactly what is and is not included so there are no surprises on cleaning day.

If a company hesitates on any of these, treat it as a signal to keep looking.

## Watch for the red flags

Some warning signs show up before you ever book. Be cautious if you notice:

1. No physical proof of insurance or bonding when asked.
2. Prices that seem too good to be true, which often means corners get cut.
3. No clear answer about who actually shows up to clean.
4. Pressure to pay large amounts in cash up front.
5. No guarantee or way to make things right if a visit falls short.

A reliable cleaner answers these questions calmly and in plain language. You should never feel like you are prying.

## Ask about supplies and safety

If you have pets, young children, or sensitivities, the products matter as much as the people. Ask what a company cleans with and whether those products are safe for your household.
