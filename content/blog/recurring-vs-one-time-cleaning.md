When you book a cleaning service, one of the first choices is whether to schedule a **one-time clean** or set up **recurring service**. Both have their place — the right call depends on your home, your routine, and your budget. Here's how they compare.

## One-time cleaning

A single, thorough clean with no ongoing commitment. It's the right choice when you have a specific reason and a specific date.

**Best for:**

- Move-in or move-out cleans
- Getting ready to host an event or guests
- A seasonal deep clean or post-renovation reset
- Trying out a cleaning company before committing

**The trade-off:** because the home may have months of buildup, a one-time clean takes longer and costs more per visit than a recurring one. You're paying for the catch-up.

## Recurring cleaning

A standing appointment — weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly — that keeps the home consistently clean.

**Best for:**

- Busy households that can't keep up
- Families with kids or pets
- Anyone who'd rather spend their weekends elsewhere
- Maintaining a home after an initial deep clean

**The advantage:** the home never falls far behind, so each visit is faster — and **priced lower**. At OCS, recurring bookings save **20% off one-time rates**, and you keep the same background-checked crew each visit, so they learn your home and your preferences.

## How most people choose

The two aren't mutually exclusive. The most common pattern is to **start with a one-time deep clean** to bring the home to a baseline, then **switch to recurring** — usually bi-weekly — to keep it there at the lower rate. You get the reset and the easy maintenance without paying catch-up prices every time.
