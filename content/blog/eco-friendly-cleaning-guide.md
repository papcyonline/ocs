Walk down any cleaning aisle and you'll see "green," "natural," and "eco" stamped on dozens of bottles. But eco-friendly cleaning is about more than a label — it's a genuinely smarter way to keep your home healthy. Here's what sets green products apart and why making the switch is worth it.

## What "eco-friendly" actually means

Not every product with a leaf on the label earns it. Truly eco-friendly cleaners share a few traits:

- **Biodegradable, plant-based ingredients** that break down safely instead of lingering in waterways.
- **No harsh chemicals** like ammonia, chlorine bleach, or phosphates.
- **No synthetic dyes or artificial fragrances** that irritate lungs and skin.
- **Transparent labeling** that lists what's actually inside.

If a label makes vague claims but hides its ingredients, treat that as a red flag. The best green products have nothing to hide.

## Better for the people and pets at home

The strongest case for green cleaning is what you stop breathing in. Conventional cleaners release volatile organic compounds and leave chemical residues on the surfaces your family touches every day — counters, floors, tables. For kids who crawl and play on the floor, and pets who walk on it and then lick their paws, those residues matter.

Switching to eco-friendly products reduces:

- Airborne irritants that can trigger headaches, coughing, and asthma flare-ups
- Skin and eye irritation from harsh fumes
- Chemical residue on food-prep surfaces and toys

This is exactly why every Ottri visit uses eco-friendly, pet- and kid-safe supplies. A clean home shouldn't come with a cloud of fumes.

## Better for your home and the planet

Harsh chemicals don't just affect your air — they can dull finishes, etch natural stone, and degrade certain surfaces over time. Gentler plant-based formulas clean effectively while being kinder to countertops, floors, and fixtures. And because they break down naturally, they don't send a stream of aggressive chemicals down the drain and into local water systems. Small daily choices add up across a whole community.

## Do green products really clean as well?

This is the question that holds most people back, and the honest answer is yes — for everyday cleaning, modern green products perform right alongside conventional ones. The trick is technique:

1. Match the product to the task instead of reaching for one harsh all-purpose spray.
2. Give cleaners a minute of dwell time to break down grime before wiping.
3. Use microfiber cloths, which lift dirt and germs with less product.

For routine messes, grease, and germs around the home, you won't miss the heavy chemicals once you've adjusted your habits.

## Simple swaps to start with

You don't have to overhaul everything at once. Begin with the products you use most:

| Conventional product | Eco-friendly swap |
|---|---|
| Ammonia glass cleaner | Vinegar-based or plant-based glass spray |
| Bleach all-purpose spray | Plant-derived multi-surface cleaner |
| Synthetic-fragrance air freshener | Open windows, baking soda, simmered herbs |
| Paper towels | Reusable microfiber cloths |

Replace items as they run out, and the transition feels effortless rather than expensive.

Eco-friendly cleaning protects the health of everyone under your roof while keeping your home looking its best — and you don't sacrifice results to get there.
